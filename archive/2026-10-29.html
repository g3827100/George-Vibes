Summary by category

Global Finance
The Fed held at 3.75%–4% on Oct 28 after September's hike, but markets price a December hike at roughly 75–83%. Prices rose 3.7% in September from a year earlier while employers added only 29,000 jobs.
Read-across: Borrowing costs are likely to stay high while inflation is driven by fuel prices.
Sources: Fox Business, ABC News, Reuters via Kitco
Stock Market
Third-quarter S&P 500 earnings are on pace to rise more than 32%, but investors are questioning about $800 billion of hyperscaler AI spending as financing costs rise. The S&P 500 is up about 12% in 2026.
Read-across: Equity sentiment depends on earnings from the AI leaders and on the rate path.
Sources: Reuters via Kitco, ABC News (AP), CNBC (headline)
Gold
Spot gold was $4,050.55 on Oct 28, down 0.2%, pressured by oil-fuelled inflation fears and rate-hike expectations. Analysts cut 2026 forecasts for a second quarter but still expect a recovery in 2027.
Read-across: Gold is trading on the rate outlook, not as a pure safe haven.
Sources: Reuters via Kitco, Bloomberg (headlines)
Oil
Brent was $111.38 on Oct 27 after a Houthi attack on a Saudi refinery, up from under $97 at the start of October. Hormuz transit restrictions and a US blockade of Iranian ports remain the main supply risks.
Read-across: Oil is the main upstream driver of inflation and therefore of the Fed and of gold.
Sources: ABC News (AP), Fox News, CNN (headlines)
